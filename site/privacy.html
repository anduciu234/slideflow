Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

This policy explains what Slideflow, provided by the Slideflow project, accesses and how it is used.

What we access

  • From TikTok, with your permission: your basic profile information (open ID, display name) and permission to upload and publish content to your account (scopes user.info.basic, video.upload, video.publish).
  • Access tokens issued by TikTok so Slideflow can post when you ask it to.
  • Content you create: slideshow images, titles, captions and your app details.

How it is used

Only to show which TikTok account is connected and to publish the content you choose, with the settings you choose. We don't use your data for advertising or sell it.

Where it is stored

Slideflow runs on your own computer. Your TikTok tokens, slideshows and app details are stored there. To publish a photo post, the slide images are uploaded to an image host so TikTok can fetch them. They are deleted from it within 2 days.

Sharing

Content is sent only to TikTok, when you post. We don't share your data with anyone else.

Your choices

Disconnect Slideflow anytime in the TikTok app (Settings and privacy → Security and permissions → Apps and services permissions) and delete your local Slideflow data. Questions: GitHub Issues.

Contact

Questions or problems: open an issue on GitHub.
