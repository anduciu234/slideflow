Slideflow

TikTok slideshows for your apps

Slideflow is a desktop studio for app makers who market their apps on TikTok. Create photo slideshows for each of your apps, preview them, and publish them to your own TikTok accounts, now or at a time you choose.

Get Slideflow on GitHub

How it works

  • Add your apps. Paste an App Store link and Slideflow fills in what your app does, who it's for and its competitors.
  • Create slideshows. Build photo slideshows in TikTok's native styles, organised by app.
  • Connect TikTok. Log in with TikTok and choose which of your accounts each app posts to.
  • Post or schedule. Publish straight to your profile, or send to your TikTok inbox to add a sound and finish in the TikTok app.

TikTok integration

Slideflow uses TikTok's official Login Kit and Content Posting API. You choose every post setting yourself, including who can view the post, whether comments are allowed, and commercial content disclosure. Nothing is posted until you click Post or Schedule.
